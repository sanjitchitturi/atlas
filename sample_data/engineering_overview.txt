SECTION 1: HEAT EXCHANGER SPECIFICATIONS
================================================================================

1.1 Overview
The Model HX-200 shell-and-tube heat exchanger is designed for high-pressure
industrial cooling applications. It transfers thermal energy between two fluid
streams without mixing them.

1.2 Operating Parameters
- Maximum operating temperature: 350°C (662°F)
- Minimum operating temperature: -20°C (-4°F)
- Maximum operating pressure: 25 bar (362 psi)
- Design pressure: 30 bar (435 psi) with a safety factor of 1.5
- Flow rate (shell side): 50–200 L/min
- Flow rate (tube side): 20–80 L/min
- Heat transfer area: 12.5 m²
- Number of tube passes: 2
- Shell diameter: 450 mm
- Material: AISI 316L stainless steel (wetted parts), carbon steel (shell)

1.3 Maintenance Schedule
- Inspect tube bundle for fouling every 6 months.
- Replace gaskets annually or when leak is detected.
- Perform pressure test at 1.5× design pressure every 2 years.
- Clean shell side with approved descaling agent quarterly.

SECTION 2: PRESSURE RELIEF VALVE (PRV) – MODEL PRV-500
================================================================================

2.1 Function
The pressure relief valve automatically opens when upstream pressure exceeds
the set pressure, diverting flow to a safe discharge path and protecting the
system from over-pressurization.

2.2 Set Pressure and Reseat Pressure
- Set pressure: 22 bar (319 psi)
- Reseat pressure (blowdown): 20 bar (290 psi) — the valve re-closes when
  pressure drops to this level.
- Maximum accumulation: 10% above set pressure = 24.2 bar

2.3 Operation Sequence
Step 1 – Normal operation: Disc is held closed by spring force.
Step 2 – Over-pressure event: When upstream pressure reaches 22 bar, the
         spring force is overcome and the disc lifts.
Step 3 – Relief: Gas or liquid is discharged through the outlet to the
         flare header or vent stack.
Step 4 – Reseat: Once pressure drops below 20 bar, spring force closes
         the disc.

2.4 Testing
PRVs must be bench-tested annually and after any activation event.
Testing procedure: ISO 4126-1 compliant pop test on certified test bench.

SECTION 3: STRUCTURAL STEEL FRAME – TYPE SF-1000
================================================================================

3.1 Material
- Primary members: ASTM A572 Grade 50 structural steel
- Yield strength: 345 MPa (50 ksi)
- Ultimate tensile strength: 450 MPa (65 ksi)
- Weld filler: E70XX electrodes

3.2 Load-Bearing Requirements
- Dead load capacity: 500 kN per column
- Live load capacity: 250 kN per column
- Wind load design: AS/NZS 1170.2 Category C terrain
- Seismic design: Zone 2A per ASCE 7-22
- Maximum allowable deflection: L/360 for live loads, L/240 for total loads

3.3 Connection Details
- Bolted connections: ASTM A325 high-strength bolts, minimum 19 mm diameter
- Welded connections: Full penetration welds inspected by UT (ultrasonic testing)
- Base plates: 25 mm thick minimum, anchor bolts ASTM F1554 Grade 55

3.4 Fire Rating
- All structural members to be protected to achieve 2-hour fire resistance
  rating per BS EN 1363-1 using intumescent paint or mineral wool cladding.

SECTION 4: DATABASE SYSTEM – FAILOVER PROCEDURE
================================================================================

4.1 Primary Database Configuration
- Primary server: DB-PROD-01 (Active)
- Standby server: DB-PROD-02 (Hot standby, synchronous replication)
- Replication lag target: < 500 ms
- Technology: PostgreSQL 15 with Patroni cluster management

4.2 Automatic Failover
Patroni monitors the primary every 10 seconds via DCS (etcd).
If the primary fails to respond for 30 seconds (3 consecutive health checks),
Patroni promotes the standby automatically.

Automatic failover steps:
1. Patroni detects primary node failure after 30 s timeout.
2. Leader election occurs in etcd cluster.
3. Standby DB-PROD-02 is promoted to primary.
4. VIP (Virtual IP) 10.0.1.100 is moved to new primary.
5. Application connection pool reconnects within 60 s.
6. Failed node DB-PROD-01 is fenced (STONITH) to prevent split-brain.

4.3 Manual Failover Procedure
Use this procedure for planned maintenance:

   Step 1: Notify stakeholders and open change window.
   Step 2: Verify standby lag < 100 ms:
           SELECT * FROM pg_stat_replication;
   Step 3: Initiate switchover:
           patronictl -c /etc/patroni/config.yml switchover --master DB-PROD-01
   Step 4: Confirm new primary:
           patronictl -c /etc/patroni/config.yml list
   Step 5: Update monitoring dashboards.
   Step 6: Close change window and notify stakeholders.

4.4 Recovery Time Objective (RTO) and RPO
- RTO: < 2 minutes (automatic), < 5 minutes (manual)
- RPO: 0 data loss (synchronous replication)

SECTION 5: ELECTRICAL SYSTEMS
================================================================================

5.1 Power Distribution
- Main supply: 11 kV, 3-phase, 50 Hz
- Main transformer: 11 kV / 415 V, 2 MVA, Dyn11 vector group
- Emergency generator: 500 kVA diesel, auto-start on mains failure within 10 s
- UPS systems: 200 kVA for critical instrumentation, 30-minute battery backup

5.2 Motor Control Centers (MCC)
- MCC-01: Process pumps (P-101 through P-115)
- MCC-02: HVAC and utilities
- MCC-03: Fire suppression and emergency systems
- All MCCs rated IP54 minimum; MCC-03 rated IP65

5.3 Cable Specifications
- Power cables: XLPE insulated, armoured, 600/1000 V grade
- Instrumentation cables: Overall screened, twisted pair, 300 V grade
- Fire-resistant cables: MICC or LSOH FR rated for emergency circuits
- Maximum voltage drop: 2.5% for power circuits, 1% for control circuits

================================================================================
SECTION 6: INSTRUMENTATION AND CONTROL
================================================================================

6.1 Control Philosophy
The plant operates under a distributed control system (DCS) with a safety
instrumented system (SIS) providing independent overpressure and high-temperature
trip functions.

6.2 Key Instruments
- PT-101: Pressure transmitter, range 0–40 bar, accuracy ±0.1%
- TT-201: Temperature transmitter, range -50–400°C, accuracy ±0.5°C
- FT-301: Coriolis flow transmitter, range 0–300 L/min, accuracy ±0.2%
- LT-401: Guided wave radar level transmitter, range 0–3 m, accuracy ±5 mm

6.3 Alarm Philosophy
- Priority 1 (Red): Immediate action required — process safety at risk
- Priority 2 (Amber): Action required within 15 minutes
- Priority 3 (Yellow): Monitoring required, action within 1 hour
- Alarm rationalization per EEMUA 191 guidelines
